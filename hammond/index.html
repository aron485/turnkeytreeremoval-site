Why You May Need Tree Removal Services

There are several reasons why you might need to consider tree removal:

  • Damaged or Diseased Trees: Trees severely damaged by storms, pests, or disease can become a danger and may spread disease to nearby healthy trees.
  • Dead Trees: Their structural integrity weakens over time, making them susceptible to falling during storms.
  • Tree Obstruction: Trees that grow too large or in inconvenient locations can obstruct views or hinder property development.
  • Storm Damage: Hammond is no stranger to hurricanes and strong thunderstorms that can leave trees leaning dangerously or losing large limbs.
  • Pest Infestations: Termites or wood-boring beetles can deteriorate a tree's health from the inside out.
  • Hazardous Tree Locations: Trees too close to power lines, homes, or structures may be safer to remove than to maintain.

Our Comprehensive Tree Services in Hammond

We offer more than just tree removal. Our services are designed to meet all your tree care needs, whether it's routine maintenance, emergency services, or improving the health and appearance of your landscape.

Tree Trimming and Pruning

Proper pruning and trimming help maintain the health and aesthetics of your trees. Removing dead or diseased branches encourages new growth, prevents safety hazards, and keeps trees from growing too close to structures or power lines.

Stump Grinding

After tree removal, the remaining stump can be an eyesore or a hazard. Our stump grinding services remove the stump below the surface, allowing you to reclaim the space for landscaping or other projects.

Emergency Tree Services

Storms or other emergencies can leave trees dangerously damaged or fallen. Our emergency tree removal services respond quickly to clear hazardous trees and restore safety to your property.

Tree Fertilization and Health Care

Not all trees need to be removed. Our fertilization services provide trees with the nutrients they need to thrive, while our pest and disease control measures keep them healthy and strong.
